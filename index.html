About Us

Best Medical Care For Yourself and Your Family

At Sundesha Healthcare, medicine is not just a profession—it is a legacy of trust, compassion, and unwavering commitment to patient well-being. Founded by Dr. Navratna Sundesha, an alumnus of Grant Medical College, the practice began in 1984 with a simple yet powerful vision: to provide ethical, dependable, and personalized medical care. Over the past four decades, he has become a trusted pillar in the community, caring for generations of families with dedication and integrity. Building upon this strong foundation, the next generation has seamlessly carried the legacy forward. With over 20 years of clinical experience, his son combines modern medical expertise with the same deeply rooted values of empathy and continuity of care. As a trusted family physician, he is committed to understanding not just illnesses, but the people behind them. Today, Sundesha Healthcare stands at the intersection of experience and modern medicine—offering thoughtful, relationship-driven care that evolves with every generation it serves.
